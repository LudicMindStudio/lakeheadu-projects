# Type of technology
intro: Start with the kind of technology you are evaluating. What it is shapes which questions matter as you go.

## What kind of technology are you evaluating?
- mHealth app
- Chatbot
- Social media
- Forum or group chat
- Something else
### If answered: mHealth app
A phone app that supports health or well-being: mood tracking, meditation, symptom journals, and similar. It may collect personal information as it works.
### If answered: Chatbot
A program that talks with you in text. It can feel like a conversation, but there may be no real person behind it.
### If answered: Social media
A platform where people post and share content. It is built to hold attention, and what people share there is often public.
### If answered: Forum or group chat
A space where a group of people talk together online, usually around a shared topic or experience.
### If answered: Something else
A game, a wearable, a website, or something different. The questions ahead still apply. Note anything specific as you go.
